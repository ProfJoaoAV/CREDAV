👤 Personal Pronouns

Os personal pronouns substituem nomes de pessoas, animais ou objetos.

Singular🔊Plural🔊
I (eu) we (nós)
you (tu) you (vós)
he • she • it
they (eles/elas)

⚠ Regra IT vs THEY:

  • it = 1 objeto ou 1 animal
  • they = 2 ou mais objetos ou animais
The dog is friendly. It is friendly. (1 cão)
The dogs are friendly. They are friendly. (2+ cães)

MÓDULO 3

📅 IN vs ON

📆 IN
in + months: in August
in + years: in 1983
🗓 ON
on + days: on Tuesday
on + dates: on 23rd December
🔑 Truque:
  • IN — coisas longas: meses, anos
  • ON — coisas curtas: dias, datas
My birthday is in May.
We have English on Monday.
