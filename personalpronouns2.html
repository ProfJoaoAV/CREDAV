👤 Personal Pronouns

Os personal pronouns substituem nomes de pessoas, animais ou objetos.

Singular🔊Plural🔊
I (eu) we (nós)
you (tu) you (vós)
he • she • it
they (eles/elas)

⚠ Regra IT vs THEY:

  • it = 1 objeto ou 1 animal
  • they = 2 ou mais objetos ou animais

MÓDULO 3

📅 IN vs ON

📆 IN
  • in + months: in August
  • in + years: in 1983
🗓 ON
  • on + days: on Tuesday
  • on + dates: on 23rd December
